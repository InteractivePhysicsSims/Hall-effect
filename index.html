CAIE 9702 Syllabus 20.2 Key Physics Steps

Exam Derivation
1. Opposite Directions Conventional current $I$ flows in direction of positive charge flow. Actual conduction electrons drift in the exact opposite direction ($v_d$).
2. Magnetic Deflection Moving charge $q$ experiences Lorentz force $F_m = q v_d B$. Electrons bend toward one face, accumulating surface charge on opposite faces.
3. Equilibrium Equation Electric field builds up until $F_e = F_m \implies q E = q v_d B$. Substituting $I = n A q v_d = n (w t) q v_d$:
$$V_H = \frac{B I}{n q t}$$
